# ReelGuard — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 25 July 2026_

ReelGuard helps you reduce the time you spend on short‑video feeds (Instagram Reels,
YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Facebook Reels, Snapchat Spotlight).

## Data collection

ReelGuard **does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data**. Your settings and
usage statistics (time spent, streaks) are stored **only on your device** and never leave
it. ReelGuard has no account system and no server.

## Accessibility Service

ReelGuard uses Android's Accessibility Service **solely to detect, on your device, when a
Reels or Shorts feed is displayed** in the apps you select, so that it can show the
blocking screen according to your settings. It does **not** read, log, or transmit the
content of your screen, your messages, or your keystrokes. All processing happens locally
on the device.

## Advertising

ReelGuard displays banner ads through **Google AdMob**. To serve ads, Google may use your
device's **advertising identifier (AD ID)**. Google's handling of this data is described at
<https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads>. You can reset or delete your advertising ID
at any time in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

## Children

ReelGuard's **Parental Mode** is intended to be configured by a parent or guardian on a
child's device. The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

This policy may be updated. Material changes will be reflected in the app and on this page.
